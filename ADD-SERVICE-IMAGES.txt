ADD SERVICE IMAGES TO YOUR WEBSITE
============================================

To complete your website, add these 4 service images to the "images" folder:

MISSING IMAGES:
--------------

1. images/cocktail.jpg
   - A photo of your cocktail bar service
   - Show cocktails being prepared or served
   - Size: At least 600×400 pixels

2. images/champagne.jpg
   - A photo of your champagne bar setup
   - Show champagne glasses or service
   - Size: At least 600×400 pixels

3. images/snack.jpg
   - A photo of your snack bar
   - Show food presentation or snack display
   - Size: At least 600×400 pixels

4. images/makeup.jpg
   - A photo of your make-up services
   - Show make-up work or installation
   - Size: At least 600×400 pixels


CURRENT STATUS:
--------------
✅ Logo - Working (images/logo.png)
✅ About image - Working (images/about.jpg)
✅ Gallery images - Working (g1-g5 in gallery folder)
❌ Service images - Need to be added

The website will display elegant placeholder graphics until 
you add the actual service photos.


HOW TO ADD THEM:
---------------

1. Take or select 4 photos showing each service
2. Rename them to:
   - cocktail.jpg
   - champagne.jpg
   - snack.jpg
   - makeup.jpg

3. Place them in the "images" folder (not the gallery folder)

4. Refresh your website - the images will appear automatically!


TIP:
----
You can use photos from your gallery folder if they show these services.
Just copy and rename them:

Example:
- Copy g1.png → rename to cocktail.jpg
- Copy g2.png → rename to champagne.jpg
- And so on...


BACKGROUND IMAGE (Optional):
---------------------------
If you have a great photo you want as the homepage background:
- Name it: background.jpg or Background.png
- Place it in: images/gallery/
- The site will use it automatically

Currently using an elegant navy blue gradient background.
